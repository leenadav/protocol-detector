How the detector sits inside Culvert.

Culvert reads the tunnel as it always does. The detector is shown the first bytes of each direction and reports what it can prove. Culvert keeps every transport and policy decision.

CONNECT tunnelCulvert relay→First bytesBounded window→Validationgo/detector→Log entrypd block

What this run shows

  • Protocols identified from payload structure, in both directions
  • Server-first protocols (SSH, SMTP) without waiting for the client
  • Honest unknown, incomplete, budget and outside-profile outcomes
  • A per-rule protocol restriction, in monitor and enforce mode
  • No client byte of a blocked protocol reaching the origin

How to read the results

  • This is a recorded lab run with synthetic traffic on loopback.
  • Signals are not a threat verdict.
  • Host names and ALPN are peer assertions, not authenticated identity.
  • One container, not two machines; no performance claim.
  • The Culvert change is a local lab branch, not proposed upstream.
