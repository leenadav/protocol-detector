Enterprise App-ID: a service splits into child apps per function; the verdict names both (app=gmail-uploading, container=gmail) and the host's catalog owner (host_owner). Go only. go/internal/childapp matches one proxied HTTP request on host, method, path prefix or pattern, query, headers, content type, direction and WebSocket; the most specific rule wins. Rule hosts must be claimed by the container or its depends_on, also at match time. Ceiling: 64 rule evaluations, 4,096 path bytes, reported in `limit`.
