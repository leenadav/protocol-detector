nightly-fuzz.yml gains a `mutation` job (30 minutes, contents: read): its one networked step builds gremlins v0.6.0 (Apache-2.0; dependencies MIT, BSD-3-Clause, Apache-2.0) from a RUNNER_TEMP copy of migration/mutation/gremlins and fills the module cache from a copy of go/go.mod and go.sum, because gremlins always runs `go mod download` and that fails under GOPROXY=off. Hashes are checked against go.sum and the checksum database, and the step runs before any repository code. `make mutation` then runs offline with MUTATION_BIN (Engine's e9b0ef0). The job fails only when a package falls more than 5 points below migration/mutation/baseline.json; the 5 points are mutation.py's TOLERANCE, a placeholder until PRO-173. build/mutation/*.json and the logs are uploaded on every run (90 days). Policy `check_mutation_build` pins the job's first four steps, its permissions and its timeout, with six self-test tamper cases. Open: the PRO-173 threshold goes to Engine.
