Go only, a divergence Lee approved on PRO-124 (the Python reference is unchanged). quicx.Assembler.Add used to forget a connection whose CRYPTO merge went over MaxFragments or MaxCrypto, so a ClientHello contradicting the complete one the connection had assembled was taken as a first message, with no conflict and no retraction. The entry now stays as a sticky exhausted marker holding its pre-overflow bytes: later datagrams answer exhausted and assemble nothing, and a contradiction of the retained bytes turns it into the conflict marker. Regression test TestCompleteThenExhaustedThenContradictionConflicts fails on main 23bbc0c and passes after; details and numbers in migration/STATUS.md and the PR.
