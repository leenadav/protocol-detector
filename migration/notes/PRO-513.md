The component's SHA-256 was computed and never checked, so an edit to childapps.json without a release bump changed rule behaviour under the same child_release, and a verdict could not name the bytes it came from. `go/internal/childapp/lock.json` is now the append-only release history (2026.11.0 to 2026.11.2, the files of e75e4a8, dadc6bd and bce8743); `Builtin` and `Embedded` refuse a component that is not its last entry byte for byte. `AppVerdict` gains `child_digest` (schema updated, additive, schema_version stays 1). Release N may have 1 to 6 digits without a leading zero and orders numerically (2026.11.10 follows 2026.11.9). `tools/child_apps_wave.py --write` pins the written release and refuses a published release with other bytes; `--check` (`make child-apps`) fails unless the lock pins the committed file. Tests: `secchild6_test.go` (5) and `TestVerdictNamesThePinnedComponentDigest`.
