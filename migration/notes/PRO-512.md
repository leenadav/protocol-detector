The loader checked only the owner of a `host_suffixes` entry itself, so `googleapis.com` claimed through `google-base` was indexed over `maps.googleapis.com` (google-maps), `play.googleapis.com` (google-play) and `remotedesktop-pa.googleapis.com` (chrome-remote-desktop). `Load` now indexes the catalog's service domains under each parent domain once and refuses a suffix whose nested domains have an owner outside the container and `depends_on`; the index is dropped when `Load` returns. Classify keeps its SEC-CHILD-2 owner re-check as defence in depth. No built-in child uses `host_suffixes`, so the release (2026.11.2) and its rules are unchanged. Tests: `TestSuffixWithForeignOwnerUnderItRefused` (refusal, acceptance with every nested owner in `depends_on`, a clean suffix) and `TestClassifyRechecksSuffixOwner` (a rule forced over a foreign host never fires there).
