The loader checked a `host_suffixes` entry only against the owner of the suffix itself (`go/internal/childapp/load.go`, `addRule`), so `googleapis.com` under `google-base` loaded although the catalog gives `maps.googleapis.com`, `play.googleapis.com` and others to other applications; only the match-time owner check of SEC-CHILD-2 (`Classify`) kept those hosts out of the child. Now `Load` indexes every catalog service domain under each of its parent domains once (`nestedDomains`), and a suffix with a nested domain whose owner is neither the container nor a `depends_on` is refused, naming the domain and its owner. The `Classify` re-check stays as the second layer. Web fronts under a suffix need no load check: `Classify` refuses a web-front host before any rule. No shipped child uses such a suffix (`TestShippedSuffixesHaveNoForeignOwners`); `TestSuffixRuleStaysInsideItsOwner` now names `www.googleapis.com` exactly. Tests: `secchild5_test.go` (refusal with domain and owner named, the finding's googleapis.com case, a nested owner listed in `depends_on` loads, the match-time re-check after the owners are tampered).

PR #201 (branch `claude/quirky-goodall-36hlwi`, `548f477`) was a second, independent fix of the same finding, opened before PR #200 merged. PRO-604 merged `main` into it: `main`'s `load.go` and `childapp.go` are kept unchanged (the two fixes do the same check), and PR #201's remaining coverage moves into `secchild5_test.go`: `TestGoogleapisSuffixLoadsWithEveryNestedOwner` (googleapis.com refused through google-base alone, loads once every nested owner is a `depends_on`, a clean `dropboxapi.com` suffix loads) and `TestClassifyRechecksForcedSuffix` (the load-time index is not kept, and a suffix rule forced over a foreign host never fires there).
