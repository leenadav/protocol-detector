title: SEC-SESS-1 in Go: half-open budget and eviction for TCP connections and undecided UDP flows
---
go/internal/session/tracker.go gives TCP connections without a completed handshake and UDP flows with no decision their own budget (MaxHalfOpen, MaxHalfOpenUDP: three quarters of the table by default) and a short lifetime (HalfOpenTimeout, 5 s, from the SYN for TCP). A new SYN or flow when that budget or the table is full evicts the oldest half-open state (new reason "Half-open budget full; oldest unestablished state evicted", counters half_open_evicted and udp_half_open_evicted); established connections and decided flows are never evicted, and a refused SYN allocates nothing. A retransmitted SYN no longer extends Time or LastSeen. Tests in go/internal/session/halfopen_test.go. Replay parity is unchanged (7,462 of 7,462), but parity-analyze (20), parity-live (6) and parity-live-decrypt (15) now differ from the frozen Python reference by design: the branch waits on Lee's PRO-397 decision (option A: the same fix in analyze_pcap.py; option B: pinned Go-only deltas). Details in migration/STATUS.md, "Security review finding SEC-SESS-1".
