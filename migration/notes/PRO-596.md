The perf-history regression of the `tls` flow (B/op +46 %, allocs 67 → 93) is mostly not a regression. Since PRO-260 (#105) the benchmark's default crypto/tls flights negotiate X25519MLKEM768, and the engine parses that ServerHello fully instead of refusing it. With the flights forced to X25519, `bdafcc9` and `main` cost the same. The remaining +3 allocs per flow, bisected to PRO-394 (#123), came from copying every ALPN offer, and this change removes them: `ClientHello.ALPNFirst` holds one copy of the first offer, made only when a fingerprint is collected, and JA4S reads the selected value in place. `BenchmarkFlow` and `BenchmarkFlowBytewise` now have explicit `tls-classical` (89 allocs, 11.74 KiB) and `tls-hybrid` (90 allocs, 14.09 KiB) cases, and the TLS allocation ceiling is 105 (was 110). The rebaseline, the benchstat tables and the explanation of the `memory.a` self-test annotation are in `migration/benchmarks/PRO-596/`. There is no detection change.
